# Demo Video Script

Target length: 2:30 to 3:00.

## 0:00-0:20 Hook

Physical security teams still turn urgent radio calls into incident reports by hand. During a real event, the supervisor needs location, severity, backup status, evidence needs, and next actions immediately.

## 0:20-0:40 Product

Patrol is a voice-first incident command assistant. A guard speaks naturally, AssemblyAI streams and understands the report, and Patrol creates a structured supervisor card with follow-up questions and dispatch actions.

## 0:40-1:40 Live Demo

1. Open the Patrol landing page and click `Launch app`.
2. In Guard View, click `Run demo script`.
3. Show the transcript and explain that the same flow works with browser microphone input.
4. Point to the new incident card in Supervisor View.
5. Highlight incident type, severity, location, backup request, summary, missing information, and suggested actions.

## 1:40-2:20 Supervisor Workflow

1. Tick one or two dispatch checklist items.
2. Click `Dispatch`.
3. Show the status badge change.
4. Click `Resolve` to demonstrate review lifecycle.
5. Explain that this creates an audit-ready trail for the report.

## 2:20-2:50 Technical Highlight

Patrol uses AssemblyAI Streaming Speech-to-Text for live guard reports and AssemblyAI LLM Gateway to extract structured incident JSON. Supabase stores incident records and review state, while Vercel hosts the Next.js app.

## 2:50-3:00 Wrap

Patrol helps security supervisors move from messy voice reports to clear incident response in seconds. Next steps are audio replay, role-based dashboards, and integrations with dispatch and CCTV systems.
